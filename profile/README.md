# BackDesk Systems

**Your back office, kept in order.**

BackDesk keeps small businesses' back office in order: stock, eTIMS invoicing, M-Pesa and bank reconciliation, suppliers,
debtors and a clear owner's report every month. We set up, customise, host and support proven open-source business software
(ERPNext), so owners can keep their till and leave the paperwork to us.

Based in Nyeri, Kenya, working with businesses across the country.

## What we do

- **Back office systems** for shops, distributors and service businesses: stock, suppliers, debtors, monthly reports
- **Contractor systems** for solar, electrical and water teams: jobs, site details, commissioning and job pay
- **eTIMS invoicing setup** with KRA
- **M-Pesa and bank reconciliation**
- **Hosting and support** for ERPNext
- **An AI assistant** inside the system that drafts quotations and answers questions about your business
- **Business websites** and **business email setup**
- **Custom software**, quoted after a short call

Prices: setup from KES 60,000; hosting and support from KES 6,500/month.

## How we build

- [ERPNext](https://erpnext.com) and the [Frappe Framework](https://frappe.io), extended with our own apps
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com) for the web front and the AI gateway
- Everything tested on throwaway copies before it reaches a client, and backed up daily off-site

Our client code and infrastructure stay private.
